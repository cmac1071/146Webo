Before

The backyard before construction — a sloped, wooded lot behind the deck, with the flat lawn between the stairs and the drop-off marked out as the future pool and patio zone. The retaining wall will run across this grade to create a level terrace, with the pool set back about 7 ft further from the deck than originally planned to make more room for the patio.

June 27, 2026

Erosion control wattle installed along the back edge of the property ahead of excavation, as required by the conservation approval.

July 2, 2026

The first full day of construction: the retaining wall trench is opened, the pool footprint is marked out on the lawn, and a crushed stone footing is laid and compacted along the back of the site.

July 6–10, 2026

A big week of progress: the retaining wall blocks are delivered, the fiberglass pool shell arrives, and the pool basin is fully excavated with a crushed stone base. Retaining wall construction gets underway and climbs to three courses with geogrid reinforcement by the end of the week.

July 13–15, 2026

The retaining wall is finished, turning its corner to fully enclose the graveled pool floor. Then the big moment arrives: the fiberglass shell is lowered into place (see the sped-up video below).

July 19–22, 2026

The shell gets backfilled with gravel and leveled out, more wall cap blocks are delivered, and plumbing lines are roughed in through the shell. By the end of the week the retaining wall is built up to its top course all the way around, though not yet capped.

July 30–31, 2026

After a short pause, work resumes: the retaining wall is finally capped, the sand filter and heat pump are installed, and utility trenches are dug for the electrical and plumbing runs. By July 31 the pool is full and swimmable — pump, heater, and filter all up and running, and it even glows at night from its underwater light.

August 4–6, 2026

With the pool swimmable, work shifts to expanding the graveled footprint beyond the original wall — grading and stripping topsoil for the patio, with an additional wall run extending the site further toward the stairs.

August 11–13, 2026

A new retaining wall — separate from the one holding back the pool — goes up along the slope that rises from the patio to the house and garage above, since the patio sits roughly 7–8 ft below the main level of the house. A run of granite steps is built into the wall, with a curved gravel sub-base alongside marking out a future concrete stairway that will tie the granite steps together. Around the pool itself, form boards go up along the full perimeter ahead of coping.
