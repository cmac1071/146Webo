Request for Determination of Applicability (RDA)

MA Wetlands Protection Act, WPA Form 1 — Upton Conservation Commission
Approved — May 27, 2026
  • Upton Conservation Commission approved the RDA in full — May 27, 2026. Negative Determination of Applicability issued; construction may proceed without a full Order of Conditions.

  • RDA confirmed as the appropriate filing — full NOI not required for this project
